Team Code of Conduct

* Teamwork: All members will contribute fairly and take responsibility for their assigned work.
* Communication: Team members will keep each other updated and raise any problems early.
* Quality of Work: Code will be tested and reviewed before being added to the final system.
* GitHub: We will use clear commits and communicate before making major changes to shared code.
* User Needs: The system will be developed with the organisation’s requirements and the needs of its users in mind.
* Usability: Reports should be clear, accurate and easy for users to understand.
* Data: Population information will be handled carefully to ensure that reports are accurate and consistent with the database.
* Professionalism: We will respect each other’s ideas and deal with disagreements constructively.
* Deadlines: Tasks will be completed within agreed timescales to avoid delaying other parts of the project.
* Punishment: If someone fails to meet the code of conduct that has been agreed on, then they must buy everyone else in the group a coffee.
